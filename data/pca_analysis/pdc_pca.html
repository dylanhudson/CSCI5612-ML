427

427 is still a lot of features- the dataset has only 678. According to the references of its Wikipedia article, the debate about the "one in ten rule" for 'events-per-variable' appears to be ongoing. Proteomics is discussed as a field with particularly high-dimensional data for which desireable EPV ratios may simply not be attainable due to the limited number of patients typically available in a cohort. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04337-2 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4777663/

This is a really fascinating and intricate problem; for the sake of efficiency in this project, let's just try to find something reasonable without losing too much variance.
