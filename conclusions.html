Conclusions

Public health and cancer research are enormously complex challenges in their own rights. Analyzing aspects of both together is perhaps even more challenging. The signals can be subtle, complex, noisy, and confounding factors are omnipresent, presenting a particularly onerous statistical challenge, to say nothing of the medical/clinical problems. As in most data analysis problems, obtaining quality data is often the first and most difficult obstacle to overcome, if it can be at all. Only with quality data can meaningful analysis and modeling be performed to extract useful signals from the noise.
